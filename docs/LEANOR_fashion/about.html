Leonor Medeiros

Fashion Designer

Hi, I'm Leonor, a passionate fashion designer with years of experience.

I love to create unique and functional designs that blend creativity, confort, and personal expression.

I work regulary on my skills in pattern making, garment construction, and fabric selection.

My personal design philosophy: fashion should be sustainable and made for real bodys.

I draw inspiration from nature and art, and enjoy pushing boundaries to create looks that are both natural and timeless. With a strong eye for balence, I’m constantly evolving as a designer while staying true to my core values of craftsmanship and sustainability.

Whether it's creating custom pieces for individual clients, collaborating on new collections, or working with teams to bring visions to life, I am always eager to take on exciting new challenges. I'm proud to be at the start of my career, where my creativity, technical expertise, and passion for fashion continue to grow.

Thank you for stopping by my portfolio. I look forward to connecting and exploring how we can create something beautiful together.

Skills & Expertise: Fashion Illustration, Garment Construction, Pattern Making & Draping, Trend Analysis, Textile Knowledge, Adobe Illustrator, Blender
